---
title: "Broken things should be replaced, they say"
date: "2026-11-02"
slug: "broken-things-should-be-replaced-they-say"
---

A leg of my chair is broken,

It tilts from one side

But I sit on its another

To balance the weight out

Trying to let it live longer

Trying to be there.

For longer.



Broken things should be replaced

They say.



I believe in repairing though.



I remember buying gum from the market

To glue the broken handle of my bat,

I remember taking out the pages 

left in my notebooks at the end of each year

And binding them together to make a new notebook for the new standard.

I remember gluing my broken spectacles and using it for months.

I remember playing few extra overs with torn up ball.



Repairing. Always.



I wonder how to repair the broken chair.

I get up to find its broken leg

The missing plastic pieces

Which adorn my memories now

Which are now lying as dust

Somewhere in the corners of house

Or dustbin 

Or sewage 

Or roads

Idk.



The pieces tangle themselves into a myriad

I look for them.

I find none.



Repair?

Replace?



The question pops up again.



I ask myself while looking in the mirror

Torn pieces of nails,

Beard,

Eyelashes which are falling down

The acne scars on the face.

I look at my forearms

Tanned with heat

Burnt with sun

And scratch marks.

The pieces of skin,

Flesh,

Falling apart.



My Heart

Falling apart.



Repair? Or replace?



I ask.



The chair creeks,

I try to balance my weight

I bend more on the opposite side

It creeks more

As I try to hold on to it for longer

I hear the cracking of the second leg

And the third

As I fall

With the fourth.



Broken pieces

Burried in my flesh.

They lie on the floor

As I lie on them.

Laughing. Crying.

Not knowing how to mend

Everything that is there on the ground.

How to pick up and build.

Piece by piece

Dust by dust.

I wonder.



Repair? Replace?



But what?

There is nothing left.
